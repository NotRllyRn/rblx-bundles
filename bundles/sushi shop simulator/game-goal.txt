the goal of the game is to help the dying business not to go bankrupt.
shop is open from 10AM to 11PM
- there is a physical clock on the wall.
at midnight there is an income statement for how the shop is doing.
there are sushi counters. to start making sushi, click on the sushi counter.
leveling up from experience will unlock recipes and better sushi
making sushi results in dirty plates
there is a sink where you can clean dirty plates
clicking on the sink results in one dirty plate being turned into a clean plate.
there is a store room that shows everyone our current supply of everything.
the store room shows how much stock is left.
there is a keymap like board showing what each emoji means to what material
there is a "7 eleven deluxe" where we can re-stock if we are low on supplies
the store allows you to purchase ingredients for sushi
to purchase ingredients, you first need to contribute by washing or making sushi
contributions will be deducted when purchasing ingredients
you can rest in the staff room
the printer shows the income statement for how the business is doing
there is a computer in the staff room with a lot of functions
handling advertisements to attract customers
adjustments to hourly pay, tip and commission can be changed at the computer.
working (washing dishes or making sushi) will make you tired
resting (sitting in the staff room) will recharge your energy bar
the sofa recovers energy the fastest for free
there is a recipe book that you can sit on the associating chair to change your recipe
you can also make coffee to restore energy faster only if there available coffee bags
there is also a tip jar in the staff room, clicking the tip jar will claim the tips in it.
employee of the day gives a tiny cash bonus at every midnight
do not go bankrupt. you do not get paid if there is no/negative money in the bank
